INVESTigator

A personal investment intelligence desktop app — a context amplifier for your own thinking, not a trading assistant. It never tells you to buy or sell.

Free & open source · MIT · your data never leaves your machine

What it actually does

🔍 ETF look-through

Decodes your ETFs into their real holdings, so you see your true company exposure — not what the ticker list implies.

🎯 Hidden concentration

Finds names you hold both directly and inside funds, plus overlap between ETFs that quietly hold the same companies.

🌍 Region & sector tilt

Grouped into blocs — Europe, Emerging Asia, North America — because a regional event hits every name in it.

📰 Noise-filtered news

Only material items, scored by how much they touch your capital, deduplicated and capped so one busy stock can't flood the digest.

🧭 Plan drift

Your real allocation versus the targets you set, so you know where the next contribution is needed.

⏰ Automatic digests

Multiple schedules with their own frequency, depth and focus. Delivered to console, Discord or email. Missed runs fire on next power-on.

The rules it plays by

  • Numbers in code, prose in the AI. The model never does arithmetic on your portfolio.
  • No raw alerts. Every item explains why it matters to you.
  • Never optimize for action pressure. No buy/sell/hold, ever.
  • Honest data. Freshness and coverage are labelled; what can't be decoded is shown as such.
  • Local and private. Holdings stay on your machine. AI is optional and can run fully offline.

Get started

Install, add what you own (just a ticker is enough), and hit Run digest. Everything else is optional.

# or run from source
python -m venv .venv
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m pip install -e ".[app]"
.\.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m investraton app

All data sources are free and public: yfinance for prices, Google News & Yahoo RSS for news, and iShares' daily holdings files for ETF look-through.
